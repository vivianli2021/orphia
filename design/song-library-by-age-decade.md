# Orphia Song Library — Curated by Resident Age Decade

Genres: Classical, Jazz & Blues, Oldies & Rock 'n' Roll, Pop, Country & Folk, Gospel & Hymns, Broadway & Musical Theater, Big Band & Swing, R&B & Soul (the 9 sign-up genres).

Era logic: each bracket is anchored to the "reminiscence bump" — songs from roughly ages 10-25 of a resident's life — computed from a representative birth year for that age bracket as of 2026. Where a genre didn't exist yet in that window (e.g. rock 'n' roll for the 90s/100+ brackets), the closest real, honest precursor is used and labeled as such rather than fabricating a song.

Each bracket lists 10 Classical pieces (one shared list, reused across all brackets — see note below) and 4 songs per remaining genre.

YouTube Link columns are **real, specific YouTube video URLs** found via web search — not generated/guessed links. For non-Classical songs, links prioritize **lyric videos that keep the original artist's vocal track** (lyrics displayed on screen over the real recording), so residents can sing along while still hearing the singer they remember — the channel **7clouds** is preferred where it has a matching lyric video. Where no lyric video exists, the link falls back to the official audio or a well-known official/studio video; for purely instrumental tracks (common among Big Band & Swing and some Jazz & Blues selections) a strong studio or live performance recording is used instead, since no lyrics apply. Classical pieces similarly link to a strong studio or live performance recording.

**Classical note**: rather than varying by decade, Classical uses one unified list of 10 of the most popular/recognizable classical pieces, reused identically across all 6 age brackets, since these works are broadly familiar across generations rather than tied to a specific era.
